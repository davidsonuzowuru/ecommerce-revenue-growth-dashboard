# E-commerce Revenue Growth Drivers Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance and identify the key drivers of revenue growth. The dashboard combines KPI monitoring, trend analysis, geographic insights, and product category performance to support data-driven decision-making.

The goal of this project is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can help stakeholders understand performance trends, identify opportunities for growth, and optimize business strategies.

---

## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of sales data every day. While this data contains valuable insights, it can be difficult for decision-makers to interpret and use effectively without proper visualization.

This project addresses that challenge by providing a centralized dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

* Monitor overall business performance
* Identify revenue growth trends
* Understand regional sales distribution
* Evaluate product category performance
* Explore insights through interactive filtering and highlighting

---

## Project Objectives

The dashboard was developed to answer the following business questions:

1. How is revenue evolving over time?
2. Which states contribute the most to total revenue?
3. Which product categories generate the highest number of orders?
4. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
5. How do order volume and revenue differ across product categories?
6. What is the overall business performance in terms of revenue, orders, and average order value?

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Summary Cards

Provides a quick overview of:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value (AOV)

### Revenue Growth Analysis

A time-series visualization showing revenue performance over time and highlighting growth trends.

### Geographic Revenue Distribution

An interactive map that displays revenue performance by state, allowing users to identify high-performing and low-performing regions.

### Product Category Analysis

Two category-level visualizations that compare:

* Top Product Categories by Orders
* Top Product Categories by Revenue

This comparison helps identify whether high-demand products also generate significant revenue.

### Interactive Dashboard Actions

The dashboard includes:

* Filter Actions
* Highlight Actions

These features allow users to explore data dynamically and uncover deeper insights.

## Key Insights

* Revenue demonstrates a consistent upward trend over the analyzed period.
* Revenue is concentrated within a small number of high-performing states.
* Certain product categories drive both high order volume and high revenue.
* Geographic performance varies significantly across regions.
* Interactive filtering enables deeper exploration of regional and product-level performance.
